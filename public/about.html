ABOUT CRAPBOOK

Crapbook is a web-based social network and creative platform founded in 2025 by Juho Hiilivirta. Crapbook is designed for artists who embrace creativity and imperfection. It allows users to draw with intentionally limited tools, share their work, and connect with others through personal artist pages that showcase their creations.

Crapbook respects your privacy. We only store the information you choose to share — such as your drawings, username, and text entries — to make the platform work. No tracking cookies, analytics, or advertising systems are used, and your data is never sold or shared with third parties.

All rights to the artwork you create and upload remain entirely yours.
