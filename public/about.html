ABOUT SKETCHBOOOK

Sketchboook is a web-based social network and creative platform. Sketchboook is designed for artists who embrace creativity and imperfection. It allows users to draw with intentionally limited tools, share their work, and connect with others through personal artist pages that showcase their creations.

 

PRIVACY & RIGHTS

Sketchboook respects your privacy. We only store the information you choose to share — such as your drawings, username, and text entries. No tracking cookies, analytics, or advertising systems are used, and your data is never sold or shared with third parties. All artwork created and uploaded by users remains their sole property.
